## Scope and limitations

- **No independent human certification is claimed.** External expert review remains pending.
- This version does not certify a product, actual E2EE provider, secure hardware or production implementation.
- Audit v2 normative/reference implementations have source and regression evidence; these are not substitutes for independently observed product behavior.
- Individual rationale coverage is 21 of 1,055 normative requirements, with further analysis tracked openly. Optional PQ MLS research is not production-approved.
- Deployment conformance requires exact scoped source, build, data-flow, cryptographic-provider and runtime evidence under the chosen profile.
